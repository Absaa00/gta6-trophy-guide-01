    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions alone: play the story first, grab the possibly-missable moments at the first chance, then grind activities, then sweep the world — and finish with 100% completion, which pops the platinum.

    Missables — do these at the first chance

    No confirmed missable list exists yet for GTA VI, and this guide invents none. The six below are the only trophies whose official descriptions point at a moment that might not come back: they need a partner beside you, a choice you can only make once, or a random event. Everything else on the list stays earnable after the story.

    Not missable

    Chapter and mission trophies (Chapters 1–4, the final mission, the mission strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone) pop from story progress and are not flagged missable. Activities, races, collectibles and heists all stay available in free roam.
